# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free community. Be constructive,
assume good intent, respect differing experience levels, and focus criticism on ideas and code.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized language or imagery,
deliberate intimidation, publishing private information, and sustained disruption of project work.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the GitHub profile. The maintainer may
edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when conduct
is harmful to the community. Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project elsewhere.
